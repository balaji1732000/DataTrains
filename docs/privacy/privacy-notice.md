## What DataTrains records

When you explicitly start an assigned task, the Collector records the visible
task screen, permitted mouse/keyboard event identifiers and timing, application
focus, the output files you select, technical environment details, and consent
evidence. It does not intentionally capture clipboard contents or reconstruct
the text you type. Recording visibly pauses when the task application loses
focus or a denied sensitive context is detected.

DataTrains also stores your account identity, organization membership, assigned
tasks, upload and processing status, reviewer feedback, and security/audit
records. The identity provider separately processes login, MFA, and account
recovery information under its own notice.

## Why the data is used

The data is used to create and quality-review computer-use trajectories for the
specific project described in your invitation and task instructions, operate
and secure the service, recover interrupted uploads, investigate incidents,
meet legal obligations, and prepare approved datasets. It must not be reused
for covert monitoring, employment surveillance, or unrelated profiling.

## Your controls

- Recording starts only after you review the task, capture boundary, and current
  consent document and then choose **Start task**.
- A persistent indicator shows when recording is active.
- You can pause immediately, and leaving the allowed application pauses
  automatically.
- You choose the output files before submission.
- Reviewer decisions and rework feedback appear in your task history.

Do not enter real passwords, payment details, health information, private
messages, or unrelated personal data during a collection task. Pause and
contact support if a task requires sensitive information or the Collector does
not stop as expected.

## Sharing and publication

Authorized reviewers and administrators for the inviting organization can
access task evidence. Infrastructure providers process encrypted service data
for hosting, identity, database, and object storage. Raw recordings remain
private review evidence. The default release contains only privacy-reviewed
trajectory/action data. An organization may optionally publish a derived video
only after a reviewer explicitly approves every segment or defines timed black
masks; the service removes audio and never adds the raw recording to the release.
This optional use requires an approved project notice and redaction policy.

## Retention, legal holds, and deletion

The inviting organization configures separate retention periods for raw
recordings, derived trajectories, and release exports. Expiry is asynchronous
and audited. An active legal hold pauses deletion. Approved privacy-erasure
requests remove eligible raw and derived objects while retaining minimal audit
and deletion tombstones needed to demonstrate the operation.
